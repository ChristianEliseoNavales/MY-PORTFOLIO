LVCampusConnect System

A comprehensive queue management and campus information system designed to streamline campus services at La Verdad Christian College.

Overview

The Challenge

Campus services at La Verdad Christian College relied on manual, paper-based queue management systems that created long wait times, disorganized workflows, and a frustrating experience for both students and staff. Additionally, visitors, new teachers, and new students frequently struggled with directions, as they were unfamiliar with the campus layout. There was no centralized platform for managing queues or providing guided campus information.

The challenge was to design and build a system that could handle real-time queue operations, provide a user-friendly public kiosk for wayfinding and information, and give administrators full control through a secure dashboard — all while being scalable enough to serve the entire campus.

Approach

The Solution

LVCampusConnect was built as a dual-interface system: a touch-friendly public kiosk optimized for 16:9 displays, and a secure administrative dashboard with Role-Based Access Control tailored for specific campus offices.

Real-Time Operations

WebSockets via Socket.io for live queue updates, window availability, and instant system-wide notifications across all devices.

Secure Access Control

Google SSO authentication with JWT and strict RBAC, ensuring each office (MIS, Registrar, Admissions) has tailored permissions.

Smart Queueing

Priority queueing for PWD and Senior Citizens, department-specific services, and thermal receipt printing for queue tickets.

Analytics & Auditing

Comprehensive analytics with dual-wave area charts, aggregated data, and detailed transaction logs for monitoring office performance.

3D Interactive Wayfinding

To solve campus navigation for newcomers, we built basic 3D floor models using Blender and created animated arrow-guide videos with Canva. These intuitive visual guides are integrated directly into the public kiosk.
