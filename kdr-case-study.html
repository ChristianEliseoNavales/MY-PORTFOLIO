KDR Titans Mobile App

A high-fidelity mobile application prototype designed in Figma to modernize the registration process for the KDR Titans obstacle course event.

Overview

The Challenge

KDR Adventure Camp (KDRAC) hosts the KDR Titans obstacle course quarterly, but the registration process heavily relied on Google Forms. This text-heavy, manual process was tedious for users, lacking the visual engagement and clear structuring that an event of this magnitude deserves.

The objective was to completely overhaul the registration experience by conceptualizing a high-fidelity mobile application prototype that prioritized an intuitive User Interface (UI) and a frictionless User Experience (UX), ultimately boosting registration conversions.

Approach

The Solution

The prototype was created over a span of a week, featuring 12 distinct panels designed for the iPhone 14/15 Pro Max form factor. It broke down the complex Google form into easily digestible steps.

Data Privacy Compliance

Integrated a dedicated Agreement Page ensuring consent collection aligned with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 prior to registration.

Category Selection

Visual selection interface allowing participants to intuitively choose between Open, Age Group, or Elite course categories.

Waiver of Liability

A mandatory waiver checkpoint ensuring participants are aware of the inherent hazards and assume personal responsibility.

Seamless Payment Upload

Integrated payment confirmation flow allowing users to upload proof of payment for administrative verification.

Results

The Outcome

The project yielded a high-fidelity prototype accompanied by comprehensive system diagrams (ERD, DFD, Use Case). By proposing this mobile-first approach, the registration flow was transformed from an administrative burden into a seamless user experience, preparing KDRAC for scalable future events.
